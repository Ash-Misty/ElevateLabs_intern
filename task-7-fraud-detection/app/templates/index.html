How to read these numbers

Why accuracy is useless here

Flagging nothing scores {{ '%.2f%%'|format((1 - dataset.fraud_rate) * 100) }} accuracy while catching zero frauds. Report recall (frauds you let through) and precision (false alarms that consume analyst time), and compare PR-AUC against the {{ '%.4f'|format(dataset.fraud_rate) }} baseline.

Isolation Forest vs Local Outlier Factor

Isolation Forest is global and near-linear — it randomly splits the space and flags points that stay isolated, so it scales to millions of rows. LOF is local: it compares a point's density with its k nearest neighbours, catching dense fraud clusters a global split misses, at roughly O(n log n) cost.

What SMOTE does, and its risk

SMOTE guarantees the model sees the minority class, but it interpolates between real fraud rows. Real fraud is rare and non-compact, so synthetic points can land off the true manifold and create a region the model trusts blindly. XGBoost's scale_pos_weight balances the loss without inventing data — both are reported side by side above.

Choosing the operating threshold

Tune on validation, confirm on test, then re-tune whenever costs or base rates change. A missed fraud usually costs far more than a manual review, so operations typically lower the threshold until review capacity binds. Always log the threshold beside the model version.
